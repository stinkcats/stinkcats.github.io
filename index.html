stinkcat Fitbit

stinkcat Fitbit is a personal app. It connects to the Google Health API with read-only permission and downloads its owner's own Fitbit data: sleep (start, end, stages), activity (steps, active minutes) and health metrics (resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, oxygen saturation, breathing rate).

What it is for

The owner uses it to track his own sleep and recovery each morning on his own computer, without copying numbers out of the Fitbit app by hand.

Who can use it

Only the owner, with his own Google account. It is not offered to the public, does not accept other users, and has no sign-up.

Privacy

stinkcat Fitbit never sells, shares or transfers any data. Read the full stinkcat Fitbit privacy policy.
