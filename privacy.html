stinkcat Fitbit privacy policy

Effective September 25, 2026. This policy covers the app named stinkcat Fitbit (home page: stinkcats.github.io).

1. Who uses the app

stinkcat Fitbit is a personal app used only by its owner, with his own Google account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. Data the app accesses

With the owner's consent, the app requests these read-only Google Health API permissions:

  • Activity and fitness (read-only): steps, active minutes, active zone minutes.
  • Sleep (read-only): sleep start and end times, sleep stages.
  • Health metrics and measurements (read-only): heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, oxygen saturation, breathing rate, skin temperature changes during sleep.

The app cannot create, change or delete any data in the owner's Google or Fitbit account.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner his own sleep, activity and recovery trends. It is not used for advertising, profiling, credit or lending decisions, or any purpose other than this personal tracking.

4. Where the data is stored

Downloaded data and the sign-in token are stored only on the owner's personal computer, in a private folder that is excluded from the owner's code repositories. No server, database or cloud service run by this app receives the data.

5. Sharing

stinkcat Fitbit does not sell, rent, share, or transfer Google user data to anyone. No third parties receive it.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

stinkcat Fitbit's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Keeping and deleting data

The owner keeps the downloaded data on his computer for as long as he wants to track his trends, and can delete it at any time by deleting the local files. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the app can no longer read any data.

8. Security

The app connects to Google only over HTTPS, requests read-only permissions only, and keeps its sign-in token on the owner's computer.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions about this app can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
